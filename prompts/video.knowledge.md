# Video knowledge

Moves that cut well:
- slow dolly-in, 4 seconds, ends on the eyes or the hands
- lateral track with the subject walking, camera at chest height
- locked-off frame with the subject entering from frame-left

Avoid in a first prompt: orbit, crash zoom, and multiple moves in one shot.

Light defaults that match the scene bot:
- teal practical as the key
- coral rim from the opposite side, thin
- no colored fog unless the student asked for weather

Emotion is written as a physical change: a breath, a glance up, a hand stopping. Not "epic" and not "sad vibe".
