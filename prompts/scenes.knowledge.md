# Scene knowledge

Character sheet order: front, three-quarter, profile. Same age, same marks, same clothes, same hair.

Hero frame defaults when the student does not specify:
- interior night: 50mm, eye-level, one practical source
- exterior day: 35mm, standing height, sun as a single direction
- product beside a person: 85mm, product and hands sharp, face slightly softer

Palette language to reuse so later bots match:
- ink black ground
- teal practical (cool)
- coral rim (warm edge only, never a full wash)

A shot that can be continued names: place, time of day, lens, and the exact action of the hands.
