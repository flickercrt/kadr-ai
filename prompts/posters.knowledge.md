# Poster knowledge

Default one-sheet: vertical, subject in the upper two thirds, empty lower third.

Title treatment defaults, set outside the image model:
- Arabic titles: a heavy geometric face, two words, right-aligned in the empty band
- Latin titles: a wide grotesque, tracking slightly open, left-aligned in the same band

If both scripts are needed, stack Arabic above Latin. Do not ask the image model to do either.

Color: the empty band stays dark enough that white or teal type will read. Do not fill that band with a face.
